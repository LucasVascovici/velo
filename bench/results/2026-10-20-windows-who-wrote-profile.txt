Who-wrote profile, Windows 11, release build, 1,000 files x 500 steps, seed 1
(the agent benchmark's workload; the `velo` adapter; 20 blame queries as in the benchmark)

Method: a throwaway script built the benchmark repo with the agent_bench adapter, then timed
the pieces. The blame pieces come from temporary Instant counters around the calls inside
commands/blame.rs run(), which were reverted afterwards and are not in the tree. The counters
cost time themselves, so the instrumented total (9.5 s) is above the uninstrumented one (7.0 s
in a separate run; 9.7 s in the first); shares are of the instrumented total.

Uninstrumented, the 20 blame() calls:   7.0 to 9.7 s over three runs (median per call 336 to 461 ms)
history(paths=[file]) x20:              0.72 s total, median 37 ms, median 5 versions of the file
read_file_at on those 5 versions x20:   0.34 s total
read_file_at, one file, all 501 snapshots: median 1.81 s (3.6 ms a read)
